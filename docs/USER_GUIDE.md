## Getting started

### Who this skill is for

Christ Schools Menu is for **parents, students, and staff** who want a quick answer to *"What's for lunch?"* without opening the school website or calendar on a phone. It reads the same public lunch calendar the school publishes on Google Calendar.

### Enable the skill

1. Open the **Alexa app** on your phone, or go to [amazon.com/alexa-skills](https://www.amazon.com/skills).
2. Search for **Christ Schools Menu**.
3. Tap **Enable**, or say:

   > *"Alexa, enable Christ Schools Menu."*

### How to talk to the skill

Every request starts with one of these patterns:

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Ask** (most common) | *"Alexa, **ask Christ Schools Menu** what's for lunch today"* |
| **Open** (hear welcome + prompts) | *"Alexa, **open Christ Schools Menu**"* |

After you open the skill, you can often shorten follow-up questions to just *"what's for lunch tomorrow"* without repeating the full skill name — but starting fresh with **"Alexa, ask Christ Schools Menu …"** always works reliably.

### Important: use the current skill name

Say **"Christ Schools Menu"** — not older names like *C.L.S. Menu* or *Christ Lincoln Schools Menu*. Those were previous versions of this lunch skill. If Alexa responds with generic advice about recipes or "check the school website," she may not have reached this skill (see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting)).

## What you can ask

All examples below assume you prefix with **"Alexa, ask Christ Schools Menu"** (or open the skill first).

### Today, tomorrow, or yesterday

| Say this | What you get |
|----------|----------------|
| *what's for lunch* | Today's menu (same as "today") |
| *what's for lunch today* | Today's menu |
| *what's for lunch tonight* | Today's menu |
| *what's for lunch tomorrow* | Tomorrow's menu |
| *what was for lunch yesterday* | Yesterday's menu |

### A specific calendar date

| Say this | What you get |
|----------|----------------|
| *what's for lunch on August seventeenth* | Menu for August 17 |
| *what's for lunch on August 13th* | Menu for August 13 |
| *what's for lunch on 8/17* | Menu for August 17 |

Alexa understands many spoken and numeric date formats. If the school has posted that day on the calendar, you will hear the menu.

### A day of the week (next upcoming)

| Say this | What you get |
|----------|----------------|
| *what's for lunch on Friday* | The **next upcoming** Friday |
| *what's for lunch Monday* | The **next upcoming** Monday |
| *what's for lunch this Friday* | The **next upcoming** Friday |

If today is Friday, *"what's for lunch on Friday"* means **today**, not next week.

### Relative weekdays — "next Tuesday" vs "this coming Wednesday"

These sound similar but mean **different days**. See [Understanding dates and weekdays](#understanding-dates-and-weekdays) for examples.

| Say this | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| *what's for lunch **next Tuesday*** | Tuesday in **next calendar week** (Mon–Sun week after this one) |
| *what's for lunch **next Friday*** | Friday in **next calendar week** |
| *what's for lunch **this coming Wednesday*** | The **next** Wednesday on the calendar |
| *what's for lunch **this Wednesday*** | The **next** Wednesday on the calendar |
| *what's for lunch **coming Thursday*** | The **next** Thursday on the calendar |

**Example phrases:**

- *"Alexa, ask Christ Schools Menu what's for lunch next Tuesday"*
- *"Alexa, ask Christ Schools Menu what's for lunch this coming Wednesday"*
- *"Alexa, ask Christ Schools Menu what's for lunch next Friday"*

### Whole week (Monday through Friday)

| Say this | What you get |
|----------|----------------|
| *what's for lunch next week* | **Monday through Friday** of next calendar week |
| *what's for lunch this week* | **Monday through Friday** of the current week |

**Example response for next week:**

> *"Christ Schools Menu for next week: Monday, no lunch planned. Tuesday, no lunch planned. Wednesday, no lunch planned. Thursday, Chicken Tenders OR Sunbutter Uncrustable and Cheese Stick. Friday, Cheeseburger OR Sunbutter Uncrustable and Cheese Stick."*

Week summaries only include **weekdays** (Monday–Friday), not Saturday or Sunday.

## Understanding dates and weekdays

All dates use **Central Time** (America/Chicago), matching the school calendar.

### "Next Tuesday" = Tuesday of next calendar week

**"Next [weekday]"** does **not** always mean the very next time that day occurs. It means that weekday in the **calendar week after the current one** (weeks run Monday–Sunday).

**Example:** Today is **Monday, August 3, 2026**.

| You ask | Resolves to |
|---------|-------------|
| *next Tuesday* | **Tuesday, August 11** (Tuesday of next week) |
| *this coming Tuesday* | **Tuesday, August 4** (tomorrow) |
| *Tuesday* or *this Tuesday* | **Tuesday, August 4** (next upcoming Tuesday) |
| *next week* | Mon **Aug 10** – Fri **Aug 14** |

**Example:** Today is **Thursday, August 6, 2026**.

| You ask | Resolves to |
|---------|-------------|
| *next Tuesday* | **Tuesday, August 11** (not Aug 4) |
| *this coming Wednesday* | **Wednesday, August 12** |
| *Friday* | **Friday, August 7** (tomorrow) |

### "This week" vs "next week"

| Phrase | Week covered |
|--------|----------------|
| **This week** | Monday–Friday of the **current** calendar week |
| **Next week** | Monday–Friday of the **following** calendar week |

On **Saturday or Sunday**, *"this week"* refers to the **upcoming** Monday–Friday (the school week starting next Monday), since the current calendar week is mostly over.

### When in doubt, use a specific date

If weekday wording confuses you, ask with a date:

> *"Alexa, ask Christ Schools Menu what's for lunch on August eleventh."*

That always maps to exactly one calendar day.

## What Alexa will say back

### When a menu exists

Single-day answers follow this pattern:

> *"Christ Schools Menu lunch for [day] is… [menu items]."*

Menu text comes straight from the calendar. You may hear options separated by **"OR"** when students can choose between entrees (for example, pizza **or** a wrap).

### When no menu is posted

> *"Sorry, Christ Schools Menu does not have lunch information for [day]."*

This means there is **no calendar entry** for that date — not that Alexa failed. Common during summer, breaks, and holidays.

### Week summary with some empty days

For *next week* / *this week*, each weekday is listed. Days without a calendar entry are spoken as **"no lunch planned"** for that day name. If **every** day in the range is empty, Alexa gives the shorter "does not have lunch information" message for the whole week.

### Calendar load errors

If the lunch calendar cannot be reached temporarily:

> *"Sorry, I had trouble loading the lunch calendar. Please try again in a moment."*

Wait a few seconds and try again.

### After most answers

Alexa asks **"Would you like to know more?"** You can:

- Say **"yes"** and ask another question
- Ask another lunch question directly
- Say **"no"** or **"stop"** to exit

Lunch information is read live from the **public Christ Schools elementary lunch Google Calendar**, maintained by the school. The skill:

- Does **not** store your personal information
- Does **not** require a Christ Schools account
- Does **not** make purchases or show ads
- Refreshes from the calendar **each time you ask**

## Troubleshooting

### Alexa talks about recipes, the school website, or "Christ Lincoln Schools on your screen"

**Likely cause:** Your request did not reach **Christ Schools Menu**. Alexa's general knowledge answered instead, or an older lunch skill responded.

**Fix:**

1. Say the full phrase: **"Alexa, ask Christ Schools Menu what's for lunch today"**
2. Confirm **Christ Schools Menu** is **enabled** in the Alexa app (Skills & Games → Your Skills).
3. Disable outdated lunch skills if you previously enabled *C.L.S. Menu* or *Christ Lincoln Schools Menu*.
4. Try again on a **school day** with a known menu (e.g. a date you see on the web calendar).

### "Sorry, Christ Schools Menu does not have lunch information for …"

**Likely cause:** No event on the calendar for that day — common during **summer break**, **holidays**, and **no-school days**.

**Fix:** Check the [official calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=christschools.org_f2uc72cd4bn3mgglvl1j49a7sk%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America%2FChicago). If the menu appears there but not in Alexa, wait a minute and retry (calendar was just updated).

### Wrong day compared to what you expected

**Likely cause:** Confusion between **"next Tuesday"** (next calendar week) and **"this coming Tuesday"** (next occurrence).

**Fix:** Use a specific date: *"on August eleventh"*. See [Understanding dates and weekdays](#understanding-dates-and-weekdays).

### Skill is slow or times out

**Likely cause:** Network issue or temporary problem loading the Google Calendar feed.

**Fix:** Ensure your Echo has Wi‑Fi. Wait a few seconds and ask again.

### "I had trouble loading the lunch calendar"

**Likely cause:** Short-lived network or calendar server issue.

**Fix:** Retry. If it persists for more than an hour, the calendar URL or hosting may need attention from the skill maintainer.

### Still having trouble?

1. Verify the date on the [official lunch calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=christschools.org_f2uc72cd4bn3mgglvl1j49a7sk%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America%2FChicago).
2. Confirm the skill name: **Christ Schools Menu**.
3. Contact the skill maintainer if the calendar is correct but Alexa consistently gives wrong or missing answers.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this the official Christ Schools app?**  
A: It is an unofficial community skill that reads the **same public lunch calendar** the school publishes. It is not endorsed by Christ Schools unless the school says otherwise.

**Q: Does it cost money?**  
A: No. The skill is free; no in-skill purchases.

**Q: Do I need to link my Amazon account to the school?**  
A: No account linking is required.

**Q: Does it work during summer?**  
A: Yes, but many summer days have **no calendar entries**, so Alexa will say there is no lunch information.

**Q: Can I ask about lunch for multiple kids or campuses?**  
A: The skill covers the **elementary lunch calendar** in the configured Google Calendar. It does not separate by student or grade.

**Q: Why does the menu say "OR" between items?**  
A: The school calendar lists choices (e.g. hot entrée **or** alternate). Alexa reads the calendar text as posted.

**Q: Can I ask about breakfast or snack?**  
A: Only what appears on the **lunch calendar** is available. Other meals are not included unless the school adds them there.

**Q: What's the difference between "next week" and asking each day?**  
A: *Next week* gives one spoken summary for Monday–Friday. Individual day questions give more detail for a single date and are better when you need one specific day.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone without an Echo?**  
A: Yes — use the **Alexa app** (iOS/Android) and speak to Alexa there, or type in the skill if your app supports it.

**Q: How often is the menu updated?**  
A: Whenever the school updates the Google Calendar. The skill fetches the latest data on **each request**; there is no manual refresh command.

**Q: I used to say "C.L.S. Menu" — what changed?**  
A: The skill was renamed to **Christ Schools Menu** for clarity. Use the new invocation: *"Alexa, ask Christ Schools Menu …"*

**Q: Does Alexa store what my child asked?**  
A: Amazon may store voice history in your Alexa account per Amazon's privacy settings. The skill itself does not collect student names or IDs. See [Privacy](#privacy).
